Statistical formats (SPSS, Stata, SAS)

Files ending in .sav, .zsav, .por (SPSS), .dta (Stata), .sas7bdat, or .xpt (SAS) are read with haven. Labelled columns are kept as-is — apply haven::as_factor() in your expression if you want to convert them to factors.

SPSS
